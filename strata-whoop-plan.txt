STRATA WHOOP — plan notes (Build 8.8.6)

Status: planned for STRATA Build 8.8.6; not built yet. The WHOOP facts below come from WHOOP's
v2 developer documentation as summarized in public sources. developer.whoop.com could not be
reached while this was written, so confirm field names, token lifetimes, and member-cap behavior
there first (step 0 of BUILD ORDER).

GOAL
- Every STRATA member can connect their own WHOOP account to their own STRATA account.
- STRATA imports that member's recovery, sleep, strain, and WHOOP workouts, then uses them to
  show today's readiness, offer a lighter session on low-recovery days, chart recovery and sleep
  next to training, and (if the member switches it on) inform Nutrition and Strata AI.
- WHOOP informs, STRATA explains, the member decides. Nothing in the plan or targets changes
  without a tap, and nothing is presented as medical advice.

ONE MEMBER, ONE WHOOP (account rules)
- A STRATA account connects at most one WHOOP account, and a WHOOP account can be connected to
  only one STRATA account (the WHOOP user id is unique).
- If the WHOOP account is already connected elsewhere, refuse: "This WHOOP account is already
  connected to another STRATA account. Disconnect it there first." Never reveal which account.
- Each connect request is tied to the member and the browser session that started it. If the
  member signs out or switches accounts before WHOOP sends them back, the callback stores nothing
  (the same rule as STRATA's existing account-changed protection).
- WHOOP tokens never reach the browser, and every WHOOP read is scoped to the signed-in member.
- Disconnect always works, even after Strata+ lapses: revoke at WHOOP, then delete the tokens and
  the imported data.
- Deleting a STRATA account revokes WHOOP access and deletes every WHOOP row with the account.
- Account export includes the WHOOP data STRATA stored, never the tokens.

WHO CAN USE IT (see OPEN DECISIONS)
- Recommended: connecting is part of Strata+, because the features that use it (Overview, Train,
  Progress, Nutrition, Strata AI) are Strata+. Free accounts see what it does and a pricing link.
- If Strata+ lapses, syncing pauses and resumes on renewal.

CONNECT FLOW (what the member sees)
1. Account → new "Connected apps" card → "Connect WHOOP" button, following WHOOP's brand
   guidelines.
2. A short consent panel lists what STRATA reads (recovery, sleep, strain and cycles, workouts,
   body measurements, first name) and what each is used for, with the privacy policy link.
3. Continue → WHOOP sign-in and approval → back on Account: "WHOOP connected. Importing your last
   30 days…", then "Last synced 2 minutes ago".
4. The card shows who is connected (WHOOP first name), last sync, Sync now, Disconnect (with a
   confirm dialog), and three switches:
   - Use recovery for workout suggestions (on by default)
   - Show WHOOP activity in Nutrition (off by default)
   - Share a WHOOP summary with Strata AI (off by default)
5. Every state has its own message: not set up on this server, not connected, importing,
   connected, WHOOP still scoring, calibrating (new WHOOP members), needs reconnect (WHOOP refused
   a token refresh), WHOOP connection limit reached (while the app awaits WHOOP approval), and
   declined at WHOOP.

WHAT STRATA DOES WITH THE DATA
1. Overview: a "Today's readiness" card with recovery % in WHOOP's bands (green 67–100, yellow
   34–66, red 0–33), HRV and resting heart rate against the member's own 30-day average, last
   night's sleep (hours and performance %), and yesterday's strain. It links to Train.
2. Train, before starting today's session:
   - Green or yellow: the normal session, with readiness shown as context.
   - Red: offer "Start a lighter session" (one set fewer per exercise, never below one, same
     weights) beside the normal Start. It applies to today's workout only, the weekly plan is
     unchanged, and the logged workout records that it was shortened for recovery.
   - Afterwards: match the WHOOP workout that overlaps the STRATA session and show its strain,
     average and max heart rate, and calories on the completion screen and in history.
   - Weight progression stays performance-based and unchanged.
3. Progress: a "Recovery and sleep" section with 8-week charts of recovery, HRV, resting heart
   rate, and sleep, plus weekly WHOOP strain next to STRATA training volume. Descriptive only, no
   cause-and-effect claims.
4. Nutrition (only with its switch on):
   - Summarize WHOOP workouts STRATA did not log (runs, rides, sports) over the last 4 weeks as
     minutes per week and an intensity from heart-rate zones, and offer them as a one-tap update
     to "Other activity" in Personal setup. Targets change only through that save.
   - Show WHOOP's daily calorie estimate beside STRATA's maintenance in See calculation, labeled
     as WHOOP's estimate. It never sets targets.
   - Offer WHOOP's height and weight when they differ from Personal setup (review first). Never
     log morning weights automatically.
5. Strata AI (only with its switch on): requests include a short summary (today's recovery band,
   7-day averages for recovery, sleep, and strain, and whether each is rising or falling). The AI
   may suggest a lighter or harder week; server validation and the medical reply-only rules stay
   as they are.
6. Admin: counts only (connected members, sync errors, WHOOP member cap), never a member's health
   data.

WHOOP API FACTS TO BUILD ON
- OAuth 2.0 authorization-code flow.
  Authorize: https://api.prod.whoop.com/oauth/oauth2/auth
  Token:     https://api.prod.whoop.com/oauth/oauth2/token
- Scopes: offline read:profile read:body_measurement read:recovery read:cycles read:sleep
  read:workout ("offline" is what grants refresh tokens).
- Refresh tokens rotate on every use: refresh one member at a time and save the new refresh token
  before using the new access token. Access tokens are short-lived; use expires_in.
- API v2 (v1 and its webhooks were retired after 1 October 2025), base
  https://api.prod.whoop.com/developer:
  GET /v2/user/profile/basic, /v2/user/measurement/body, /v2/cycle, /v2/recovery,
  /v2/activity/sleep, /v2/activity/workout (plus by-id reads); DELETE /v2/user/access revokes.
  Sleep and workout ids are UUIDs. Collections page with start, end, limit (1–25), nextToken.
- Webhooks (v2): recovery.updated/deleted, sleep.updated/deleted, workout.updated/deleted. They
  carry ids, not data (a recovery event carries the id of its sleep); STRATA then reads the record.
  Signature: X-WHOOP-Signature = base64(HMAC-SHA256(X-WHOOP-Signature-Timestamp + raw body,
  client secret)). Reject bad signatures and timestamps more than 5 minutes off.
- Rate limits: 100 requests per minute and 10,000 per day for the whole app, all members together.
- Scores can be SCORED, PENDING_SCORE, or UNSCORABLE, and new WHOOP members may be calibrating.
- A new app is limited to 10 WHOOP members until WHOOP approves it (privacy policy URL, designs
  that follow the brand guidelines, tested with at least one member). Community reports describe
  approvals taking weeks to months, so submit as soon as the connect flow works.

SYNC
- On connect: import the last 30 days (about 10 requests per member) and body measurements.
- Webhooks keep data fresh: verify, answer quickly, drop duplicates, then read just that record;
  a *.deleted event removes it.
- Safety net: a daily re-check of each member's last 3 days, "Sync now" (at most every 5 minutes),
  and a quiet refresh when a member opens Overview with data older than 6 hours.
- One app-wide limiter (about 90 per minute and 9,500 per day, leaving headroom) serves, in order:
  webhook reads, Sync now, first imports, daily re-checks. On 429, wait for Retry-After and back
  off.
- Budget: about 12 requests per member per day, so roughly 800 connected members fit in the daily
  limit. Ask WHOOP for a higher limit well before that.
- Jobs are saved in the database so a restart resumes them (STRATA runs as one process, like the
  Strata AI queue).
- A refused refresh (revoked in the WHOOP app, or expired) marks the connection "needs reconnect"
  and nothing else breaks.

DATA STORED (only what the features use)
- whoop_connections: member, WHOOP user id (unique), first name, granted scopes, encrypted tokens
  and expiry, status, the three switches, consent time and version, last sync and last error.
- whoop_oauth_states: one-time connect states, stored hashed, tied to member and session,
  10-minute expiry.
- whoop_cycles, whoop_recoveries, whoop_sleeps, whoop_workouts, whoop_body: the scores and metrics
  above, with start, end, timezone offset, score state, and WHOOP's updated time.
- whoop_sync_jobs and whoop_webhook_events: kept briefly, for retries and duplicate detection.
- Every row belongs to a user id with ON DELETE CASCADE. Keep up to 13 months while connected, and
  delete on disconnect (see OPEN DECISIONS).

SECURITY AND PRIVACY
- Tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM, key in WHOOP_TOKEN_KEY, key id stored so the key can
  be rotated) and are never logged, exported, or sent to the browser.
- Connect starts with a CSRF-protected POST that returns WHOOP's URL, and the page then navigates
  there; the site's content security policy only lets forms post to STRATA itself.
- Connect state: 32 random bytes, stored hashed, single use, tied to member and session, 10 minutes.
- Webhooks: raw-body HMAC check in constant time, 5-minute window, events for unknown members
  ignored.
- Logs stay content-free (whoop.connected, whoop.sync_failed with an error code), like
  ai.unreadable_answer.
- Privacy policy and terms get a WHOOP section: what is read, why, consent, retention, deletion,
  the Strata AI summary only when switched on, never sold, and how to revoke in STRATA or in the
  WHOOP app. WHOOP data is informational, not medical advice.
- Follow the WHOOP API Terms of Use and brand guidelines, and confirm the terms allow sending the
  opt-in summary to STRATA's own model server.

BUILD PLAN (code)
- Server modules, each added to architecture-policy.json:
  whoop-config (environment and preflight), whoop-crypto (token encryption), whoop-client (OAuth,
  paged reads, limiter, retries), whoop-schema and whoop-store (tables and queries for SQLite and
  Turso), whoop-core (pure mapping, units, baselines, readiness bands, lighter-session rule),
  whoop-sync (import, webhook jobs, daily re-check, one refresh at a time per member), and whoop
  (routes).
- Routes:
  GET    /api/integrations/whoop                  status and switches
  POST   /api/integrations/whoop/connect          returns the WHOOP authorize URL
  GET    /integrations/whoop/callback             finishes connecting, then redirects to Account
  POST   /api/integrations/whoop/sync             Sync now (throttled)
  PUT    /api/integrations/whoop/settings         the three switches
  DELETE /api/integrations/whoop                  revoke, then delete
  GET    /api/whoop/readiness, /api/whoop/trends  data for Overview, Train, and Progress
  POST   /webhooks/whoop                          signed WHOOP events
- Browser: new modules for the Account card, the Overview readiness card, the Train readiness line
  and lighter-session start, and the Progress charts, each within
  frontend-architecture-policy.json. discover.js is at its size limit, so nothing new goes in it.
- Workout records gain an optional "adjusted for recovery" marker that history and export show.

TESTS
- A fake WHOOP server (like the fake AI model): authorize page, token exchange with rotation, v2
  collections with paging, revoke, signed webhooks, 429s, and the 10-member cap.
- Unit: encryption and tampering, connect-state lifecycle, signature checks, score states and
  calibrating, naps, timezone offsets, kilojoules to kcal (÷ 4.184), readiness bands, limiter and
  backoff.
- Server: connect and callback, expired or reused state, changed session, WHOOP account already
  connected, two members kept apart, disconnect and account deletion revoking and deleting, export
  without tokens, lapsed Strata+, webhooks for unknown members.
- Browser journeys: connect through the fake WHOOP, readiness card on phone and desktop, lighter
  session start, history match, disconnect, and an axe sweep of every new state.
- Load: 100 connected members and a webhook burst stay within 100 requests per minute and lose
  nothing.

SETUP (my side)
- Create a WHOOP developer account and an app named STRATA at developer.whoop.com.
- Redirect URL: https://<my domain>/integrations/whoop/callback
- Webhook URL (v2): https://<my domain>/webhooks/whoop
- Scopes: offline, read:profile, read:body_measurement, read:recovery, read:cycles, read:sleep,
  read:workout. Fill in the privacy policy URL and contact email.
- Render environment: WHOOP_CLIENT_ID, WHOOP_CLIENT_SECRET, WHOOP_TOKEN_KEY (32 random bytes,
  base64), and optionally WHOOP_REDIRECT_URI (defaults to APP_BASE_URL + the callback path).
- Test with my own WHOOP first (the app starts limited to 10 WHOOP members), then submit the app
  for approval.

OPEN DECISIONS (recommended option first)
1. Who can connect: Strata+ members only / every account.
2. On disconnect: delete the imported WHOOP data / keep the history.
3. First import: 30 days / 90 days (about 3 times the requests).
4. Red recovery: offer a lighter session for today only / also propose weekly plan changes.
5. Nutrition: WHOOP calories as evidence only / let them change calorie targets.
6. Strata AI: off until the member switches it on / on as soon as WHOOP is connected.
