# STRATA — one-page overview

**Your next workout, ready. Your last workout, remembered.**

## The problem

People who already lift keep their routine in notes, a spreadsheet, or their head. Two questions come up every
session: *what am I doing today*, and *what did I lift last time?* Generic apps answer with long exercise lists or
programmes that ignore the equipment and days a person actually has, and most exercise advice online gives no reason
for its picks.

## Who it is for

Recreational lifters who know basic technique and train two to four times a week, at a gym or at home. They want a
plan that fits their week and a log that shows last time's numbers while they train. They are not looking for a coach
or a medical programme.

## What STRATA does

- **Ranked exercises.** 320 resistance-training exercises (71 bodyweight) across 8 muscle groups and 26 targets, each
  with a FitScore and the reasons and sources behind it (How FitScore works, on the home page at `/#method`). Members' ratings sit beside
  the score.
- **A weekly plan** built from those rankings, the equipment someone has, and the days they train.
- **Training Memory.** While logging a set, the sets, reps, and loads from the last comparable session are on screen,
  and the next session's progression is suggested from them.

## Free

The rankings with their guides, a weekly planner that works without an account and syncs once you sign in, the basic
profile, and the account itself. STRATA is useful before anyone pays, and a member's plan stays theirs if they stop
paying.

## Strata+ — $2.99 USD a month

Everything in Free, plus:

- **Train:** workout logging with Training Memory, progression suggestions, optional check-ins, and training blocks.
- **Plan ahead:** personalised rankings, comparisons, session building, and a 31-day planner.
- **Nutrition:** calorie and macro targets from the same personal setup, a daily diary, and meal ideas.
- **Recovery:** connect a Polar device to see Nightly Recharge, sleep, and heart rate beside training, and get an
  optional lighter session after a poor night.
- **Strata AI:** describe a week in plain words and get a plan built from all 320 exercises, checked by STRATA before
  it appears; a Daily Brief each morning. Nothing changes until the member applies it.

One membership, however it is bought: on the web through Paddle (merchant of record, so tax and receipts are handled),
or in the STRATA iOS app through Apple In-App Purchase. There is no free trial; cancel any time.
